\section*{TL;DR (revised after report 12)}

\textbf{Headline checkpoint revised.} The previous report-07 ckpt
(\texttt{best\_deeplabv3plus\_keypoint.pth}, single-gate target) was
shown by report 12 to have memorised \texttt{flight-01p-ellipse}; on
the only piloted flight held out from SAM training
(\texttt{flight-06p-ellipse}, 0 train crops) it produces 0\,\% detection.
The Stage~2 sibling (\texttt{best\_deeplabv3plus\_keypoint\_multi.pth},
multi-gate Gaussian targets + \texttt{coherent\_quad\_decode}) generalises
and is the new deployment checkpoint, delivering \textbf{78.9\,\% detection rate}
at \textbf{1.30\,px median} / \textbf{61.0\,\% within 5\,px} on the same
held-out flight, single forward pass, no prior, no EKF state, \textbf{0.44\,ms
TRT FP16 / 2274\,fps on an RTX 5090}. Upper-bound false-positive rate on no-gate
frames of the held-out flight: \textbf{2.4\,\%} (4/166 at conf $\geq 0.3$).
What is \emph{not} yet measured: bench on the autonomous held-out flights
in SAM val (7 \texttt{XXa} flights), real-recorded footage (blocked on
Drive data), bootstrap CI on the reported percentages.

\section{What works today}

Numbers are on \texttt{flight-06p-ellipse} (TII v3.0.0 piloted, \textbf{held-out --- 0 SAM training crops},
374 frames sampled at stride 30, seed 42), reproducible from
\href{run:../12_cross_flight_held_out/findings.md}{report 12}.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline,itemsep=2pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item[Segmentation backbone.]
    DeepLabV3+ with ResNet-34 + ImageNet pretrain --- val IoU \textbf{0.872}.
    \hfill {\small\itshape report 05 / 07}
  \item[Keypoint head.]
    Multi-task 4-channel Gaussian-heatmap head, sub-pixel decode via
    soft-argmax. \hfill {\small\itshape report 07}
  \item[Deployment-mode bench (\texttt{model\_direct}, held-out 06p).]
    \textbf{78.9\,\% detection} / \textbf{1.30\,px median} / 61.0\,\% within 5\,px
    on flight-06p-ellipse (no GT prior, no EKF state, 0 SAM training crops).
    \hfill {\small\itshape report 12}
  \item[\texttt{gt}-prior bench on the same held-out flight.]
    Confirms seg head transfers: \textbf{95.8\,\% within 5\,px} (perfect-prior
    QuAdGate refinement) --- the seg head learned generalisable features
    from the SAM-mask pipeline.
    \hfill {\small\itshape report 12}
  \item[False-positive rate (upper bound, held-out 06p, conf $\geq$ 0.3).]
    \textbf{2.4\,\%} (4/166 sampled no-gate frames). Upper bound because
    labellers skip non-trivial frames --- a ``fire'' may be detecting an
    unlabelled gate.
    \hfill {\small\itshape report 12}
  \item[Fire-condition tightening.]
    \texttt{coherent\_quad\_decode is None} no longer falls through to
    soft-argmax; the predictor returns a non-detection cleanly (CLAUDE.md §6).
    Cost: 2/297 lost detections; structural FP win (no Frankenstein quads).
    \hfill {\small\itshape task 3, this turn}
  \item[Prior-mode framework.]
    Six priors wired in one bench script: \texttt{gt}, \texttt{noisy},
    \texttt{none}, \texttt{model}, \texttt{model\_direct}, \texttt{ekf}.
    \hfill {\small\itshape reports 06 / 07 / 08}
  \item[TRT FP16 export (multi-gate ckpt, the new headline).]
    Multi-task graph exports to ONNX (opset 18) and builds an FP16
    TRT engine in $\sim 7\,$s. \textbf{0.44\,ms / 2274 fps on RTX 5090},
    projects $\sim$50--100\,fps on Jetson Orin NX.
    \hfill {\small\itshape \texttt{bench\_trt\_keypoint.py}}
  \item[TRT numerical correctness.]
    TRT FP16 output matches torch FP32 to
    $|trt - torch|_\infty < 1.3\!\times\!10^{-3}$ (seg) / $2.4\!\times\!10^{-4}$ (kp)
    on uniform-$[0,1]$ input.
    \hfill {\small\itshape same}
  \item[Segmentation backbone shared.]
    DeepLabV3+ with ResNet-34 + ImageNet pretrain --- val IoU \textbf{0.872}
    on SAM-cropped val. NB: this is per-crop IoU on the SAM val pool
    (mostly 06p crops), not full-frame IoU on raw flight images.
    \hfill {\small\itshape report 05 / 07}
\end{description}

\paragraph{Knock-on consequences ruled in.}
The architecture exports cleanly to TRT; multi-gate-target training
generalises across flights (report 12); the deployment headline is
now on a truly held-out flight, not an over-trained one. The
single-gate ckpt (\texttt{best\_deeplabv3plus\_keypoint.pth}) is
retained as a sibling for any future flight-specific use case but is
not the deployment ckpt.

\paragraph{What the open tests do \emph{not} cover.}
``Which gate is the next one to fly through'' is not a perception question
under the current architecture --- it is a planner-side concern that needs
an EKF + track-plan + plane-test (the MonoRace recipe, Bahnam et al. 2026).
That workstream is items 4--6 on the master rank list (Kalman fusion,
ROS2, Orin NX) and intentionally outside the scope of ``are gates
detected reliably.''

\section{Recommended order}

The original items 1--3 from this status note (cross-flight bench,
FP-rate measurement, multi-gate sibling cross-flight) were done in
report 12 and this turn's task batch. Headlines were revised accordingly.
The revised next steps:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item[\textbf{1.}] \textbf{Item 6 (class-balanced retrain)} addresses
        the \emph{root cause} of report 12's findings: the SAM training
        pool over-represents 01p-ellipse 3$\times$, which is why the
        single-gate ckpt memorised it. Cap per-flight at $\sim$300 crops
        and retrain both ckpts. \textasciitilde 1 day.
  \item[\textbf{2.}] \textbf{Item 1 (autonomous held-out bench)} ---
        closes the held-out picture beyond just 06p. \textasciitilde 1 h.
  \item[\textbf{3.}] \textbf{Items 2--4 (CI + visual inspection + per-flight
        IoU)} --- statistical rigour + attribution. \textasciitilde 2--3 h
        combined.
  \item[\textbf{4.}] \textbf{Item 5 (confidence calibration)} only if
        items 3--4 show the remaining 2.4\,\% FP rate is a real precision
        loss, not labeller-skipped real gates. \textasciitilde 2 h.
\end{enumerate}

Items 7--9 unblock once the Drive data lands on qolam; item 10 is the
only remaining unblocked retrain lever and only justifies its cost if
held-out flights show motion-blur-specific failures.

\paragraph{Bottom line.}
The model detects gates with \textbf{78.9\,\% recall / 1.30\,px median /
2.4\,\% upper-bound FP rate} on the only piloted flight held out from
SAM training, and we have enough plumbing (TRT export verified, prior-mode
framework, two checkpoints) to ship. The single biggest remaining lever
is the class-balanced retrain (item 6, root cause from report 12) ---
everything else is incremental.
